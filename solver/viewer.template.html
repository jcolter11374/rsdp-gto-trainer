How to read this. Each cell is split by how often the solver takes each action with that hand. Faded cells are hands that rarely reach this decision.
  • What is calculated: hand-against-hand equities, pot odds, card removal, and the balanced strategy for every seat (found with CFR+, the method commercial solvers use).
  • What is assumed: play after the flop is not simulated. A hand's share of the pot is its equity adjusted for position, initiative, playability and stack depth, with settings tuned so overall widths match typical published 100bb ranges.
  • Simplifications: every pot is heads-up (a caller or 3-bettor pays a fixed risk for the players still to act), the SB raises or folds, and there is one bet size per decision.
Trust the clear cells. Treat mixed and borderline cells as close decisions that could go either way.
